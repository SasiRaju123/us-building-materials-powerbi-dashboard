# US Building Materials Sales Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, product categories, customers, sales channels, and external market indicators in the US building materials industry.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of:
- Total Revenue
- Total Units
- YTD Revenue
- Average Selling Price
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue by Customer Type
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Channel

### 2. Sales & Time Analysis
Analyzes:
- Monthly Revenue Trends
- Year-over-Year Revenue
- Year-over-Year Units
- Current vs Previous Year performance
- YTD Revenue

### 3. Product & Category Performance
Analyzes:
- Revenue by Product Category
- Revenue Contribution by Category
- Units Contribution by Category
- Top 10 SKUs
- Active SKUs
- Revenue per SKU

### 4. Customer & Channel Intelligence
Analyzes:
- Revenue by Customer Type
- Units by Customer Type
- Revenue by Channel
- Revenue per Transaction

### 5. Market Context
Compares sales performance with:
- Housing Starts Index
- Lumber Price Index
- Mortgage Rate
- Season Factor

## Data Model

The dashboard uses a star-schema-based model containing:

- Fact Transactions
- Date Dimension
- Product Dimension
- Region Dimension
- Channel Dimension
- Customer Dimension
- Macro Drivers

## Key Features

- Interactive slicers
- Year/Month bookmark navigation
- KPI cards
- Time-series analysis
- Product and customer analysis
- Market-context analysis
- Interactive cross-filtering
- Professional dashboard design

## Business Questions

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How is revenue performing over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which SKUs are the top revenue contributors?
- Which customer types generate the most revenue?
- How does revenue differ across sales channels?
- How does revenue per transaction vary by customer type?
- How do external market indicators move alongside sales?

## Disclaimer

The dashboard is intended for analytical and portfolio demonstration purposes.

Market indicators are used for contextual analysis and do not imply causation.
